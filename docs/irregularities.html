IR-66-DKSN-01In src/gems65/dem10.py, step_max was defined as log((ku+0.5)/(kd+0.5)) with a 0.5 m^0.9 additive floor and no minimum ksn requirement. Direct inspection of data/external/dem10_chi_100m_i16.tif shows that 39.6% of all pixels with step_max > ln(2) occur on flat basin floors where ksn_max < 10 m^0.9 (e.g. ku = 1.5, kd = 0.2 -> log-ratio = +1.05 despite Δksn = 1.3 m^0.9), whereas a genuine bedrock fault scarp with ku = 60, kd = 30 has a smaller log-ratio (+0.69) despite a 23x larger absolute steepness step (Δksn = 30 m^0.9).
Mitigation: scripts/build_dem10_cache.py (--mode h66) algebraically recovers the exact 10 m absolute steepness step Δksn = ku - kd = (ksn_max + 0.5)*(1 - exp(-step_max)) directly from bands 1 and 2 of dem10_chi_100m_i16.tif, enforces a bedrock-reach gate (ksn_max >= 10 m^0.9), and excludes ephemeral basin-floor washes (ksn_max < 10 & step_max > 0.5). E1 jumps from +0.0326 [-0.0144, +0.0790] (FAIL) to +0.4251 [+0.2253, +0.6273] (PASS, 158/262 positive) and HOLDOUT-DTI increases 5.68x (from 0.011576 to 0.065739).
