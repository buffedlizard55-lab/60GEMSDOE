Metric decision rule

For a candidate unit-mass dot with maximum kernel weight q and actual incremental weighted truth credit ΔT, its general improvement condition is ΔT(1−αs) > αs(1−q). Only for the nonredundant special case ΔT = q = k does this reduce to k > αs. The coefficient αs/(1−αs) belongs to the false-mass term in score inversion, not the general single-dot cutoff.
