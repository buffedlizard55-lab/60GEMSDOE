The method, in one page

1. Absolute elevation (the thing the family never had)

The official 19-band stack carries a detrended elevation only. A χ transform cannot be computed from it. This session obtained the GeoDAWN absolute DEM (22103_dem_a2.tif, 100 m, the same 3730 × 3292 grid as the competition rasters) from the public jklinck/geothermal_research repository and verified alignment cell by cell. sha256 d32762a38222764389e948da423ebc6cadd00ac617ad3938d3dd79c6cfe14431.

2. Hydrology

Priority-flood depression filling (Barnes et al. 2014) then D8 routing (O'Callaghan & Mark 1984), then drainage accumulation. Verified against independent brute-force implementations on random DEMs. Measured here: maximum accumulation 1,287,069 px, 899,623 cells filled (17.4 % of the valid grid — the Great Basin's internal drainage, the named playa confound).

3. χ and normalised steepness

χ(x) = ∫x_bx (A₀/A(x′))θ dx′ with θ = 0.45 (Wobus et al. 2006). In χ–z space the profile slope is the normalised channel steepness ksn = (U/K)^(1/n) (Perron & Royden 2013). A fault crossing a channel puts a base-level step there: a break in the χ-profile.

4. Knickpoints by reproducible segmentation

For every candidate breakpoint in the middle of a sliding window, fit two lines and compare with one line. The segment contrast C = (SSE₁ − SSE₂)/SSE₁ is maximised over the breakpoint; local maxima above a declared threshold are knickpoints, with declared window and separation. This is the explicit, parameter-declared form of the Gailleton et al. (2019) segmentation.

5. Multi-drainage alignment: channel-normal casting

A fault trace crosses a channel roughly perpendicular to it. Each candidate crossing therefore casts a short segment along the local channel normal, weighted by its step magnitude. Where several independent drainages cast across one common strike, the casts reinforce and a continuous fault trace emerges. Crossings that belong to no common strike cancel out. This is the operational form of the lane's requirement.

6. Holdout

Buffered whole-component hide-and-recover: the catalogue is split into 8-connected components, each assigned whole to one spatial quadrant, visible catalogue within 3 px (300 m) of a withheld component removed, emission over the whole raster, and the score pooled over folds with a paired 512-px block bootstrap. 56,619 withheld positive pixels.

7. Emission

The metric's own optimum: peaks of the field at ≥ 2 px separation, taken in decreasing order of value up to the budget that maximises the pooled HOLDOUT-DTI.

Every number on this site is labelled HOLDOUT-DTI (evaluator version, number of withheld positives, 95 % CI), ORGANIZER-PUBLISHED, OWNER-REPORTED or MEASURED. A projection is never written as a score. Nothing here has been submitted to the competition by this project.
