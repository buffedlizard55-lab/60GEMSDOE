Executive Summary

The goal of this project is to discover hidden geothermal vents using multi-modal geophysical and topographic data for the DOE GEMS competition. We are testing the H61 Tectonic-Geomorphology Hypothesis.

Rather than relying solely on high-resolution LiDAR scarps (which degrade over time), this hypothesis leverages the idea that channel profiles persistently record base-level steps. We extract local valley networks and compute channel steepness, searching for collinear knickpoints (steps in steepness) that align across multiple independent drainages.

This approach gives us a unique signal uncorrelated with standard edge detectors and catalogue-heavy ML models, bringing true scientific discovery to the forefront.

How to Make a Submission

Follow these exact steps to submit our generated TIF file into the competition:

  1. Download the file: Go to the Home page and click the green "Download TIF Submission" button. The file will save as h61-geomorph-submission.tif.
  2. Navigate to DrivenData: Go to the Competition Submission Page. Ensure you are logged into your account.
  3. Upload: Click "Choose File" under the "File to submit" section and select the h61-geomorph-submission.tif you just downloaded.
  4. Add Note (Optional): In the Note section, add a short comment to identify it, such as: "H61: collinear steepness steps across drainages".
  5. Submit: Click the submit button.
    Note: Our file correctly guarantees all predicted values are in the range [0, 1] and matches the exact CRS, shape, and geotransform required.
