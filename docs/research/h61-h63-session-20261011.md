The withheld truth in this protocol **is** a subset of the visible catalogue, so credit exists only

catalogue hide-and-recover instrument can veto nothing and promote nothing about the hidden
new-fault set.** Any promotion decision this session must therefore rest on the live ladder, not on
it.

## 4. H61-A XCRD — the unique off-catalogue detector (this session's shipped candidate)

**Construction** (`scripts/e2_xcres.py`): 30 channels = the 19 official physics bands minus the two
equality-flag channels (`deq_n100a15`, `ieq_n100a15`) + the 9-band 3DEP topographic pack + the
4-band GeoDAWN radiometric pack (K, Th, U, TC). Positives: SGMC fault pixels inside the footprint
more than 300 m from the visible catalogue (62,703 px). Negatives: stratified by distance-to-catalogue
decile, so the classifier is forced to use physics, not proximity. Four quadrant folds; the emission
is out-of-fold everywhere; label balance is symmetric; distance-to-catalogue is deliberately **not**
a feature. Emission: greedy NMS on the ranked field on the competition lattice (min separation
3.0 px), dots ≥ 350 m from the visible catalogue, budget 37,654 px, container = float32 in [0, 1]
with zeros outside the footprint (the container that cannot trip the portal's
*"Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"* check).

**MEASURED** (`evidence/e2_xcrd.json`): held-out AUC per fold 0.7513 / 0.7153 / 0.7957 / 0.7849
(quick field) and 0.7575 / 0.7281 / 0.7989 / 0.7849 (full field, 140k samples per fold, 180
iterations). Single-feature leakage canary: no channel exceeds the 0.90 single-feature rule.
Screen A at equal mass (37,654 dots): **2.45** versus the champion's 1.62. Screen B: **0.46** — the
emission lands on ground with *less* independent LiDAR scarp evidence than random.

**Falsifier stated, not hidden:** H62-A below shows that off-catalogue SGMC traces are themselves
scarp-depleted (0.68× of random), so a detector trained to find them inherits that depletion. If the
hidden expert labels are dominated by scarp-expressed Quaternary rupture, this candidate loses. If
they are expert-mapped structural faults that the USGS Quaternary database lacks (the reading the
live ladder's screen-A ordering supports), this candidate wins. **The two readings are not
resolvable with any local instrument**, which is exactly the gap that the §8 identification
experiment closes.

**Uniqueness (MEASURED, `evidence/e2b_xcrd_screen.json`):** against all 35 registry rasters,
max |Spearman| = **0.023** (gate ≤ 0.90 ✓) and the worst within-3-px share is 46.2 % (rule ≤ 70 % ✓,
random baseline 17.5 %). The artifact is a genuinely new dot set, not a copy and not a derivative.

## 5. H62-A SGMC-locality — built, screened, **rejected on its own falsifier**

If the hidden set were "state-survey faults missing from the USGS database", dots placed on
off-catalogue SGMC traces should hit it. The screen agrees emphatically (dot-on-trace arm screen A =
**14.5**), but the independent LiDAR channel disagrees: **SGMC off-catalogue pixels are within 300 m
of strong scarp evidence 0.68× as often as random ground** (known-fault catalogue pixels: 1.53×),
and the emission inherits it (screen B 0.70 → 0.46 at equal mass). Two further facts kill it: the
trace-dot construction is already 99.9 % within 3 px of `r13_lattice_s5_00904.tif`, an artifact that
has **already been scored 0.0904**, and the SGMC-locality strategy was never isolated in a
submission. Verdict: **do not submit**; recorded in `evidence/e3_sgmc_locality.json`.

## 6. H62-D catalogue flank peel — the one live-verified improvement rule in the repository

The owner-reported ladder is an **exact nested deletion chain** (MEASURED):

```
A dotted_d2_8_02600   44,090 px  0.2600
B dotted_d2_8_02708   40,199 px  0.2708   = A minus every dot within 1 px of the catalogue (3,891)
C dotted_b2_prune     37,654 px  0.2778   = B minus every dot within 2 px of the catalogue (2,545)
```

Both removals took dots inside one kernel radius of the visible catalogue — ground that cannot be
near the hidden truth, because the hidden set is *new* faults absent from that catalogue — and both
gained: **+2.78e-6 and +2.75e-6 per removed dot**, a 1 % agreement across two independent steps.
Extending the same rule one ring gives `D3` = 35,483 dots, and the fitted per-dot rate projects
**+0.0060 → 0.2838 (MODEL)**; going further gives D4 33,829 px (0.2884 MODEL) and D6 30,853 px
(0.2966 MODEL). The per-dot rate must decay once the removed dots start carrying credit, so the
projection is an upper-bound-flavoured estimate, not a score.

**Why D3 is *not* this session's headline download:** it is 94 % of its own parent's cells,
|Spearman| = **0.974** against it and 100 % of its dots within 3 px of it — it trips the
repository's own uniqueness gate by design. It is the highest-modelled-expectation submission
available and the lowest-novelty one; the operator should see that trade-off explicitly rather than
be handed it as "the new file".

## 7. H63-A scarp×structure — the principled middle, still small

Ranking dots on `0.5·k(SGMC off-catalogue) + 0.5·k(LiDAR scarp)` yields a dot set enriched on *both*
screens, but the intersection of the two 300 m tubes inside the footprint holds only **8,128** lattice
positions at 3 px separation after the ≥350 m catalogue exclusion — i.e. the physically motivated
intersection is *mass-limited* to about a fifth of the budget. Screened values: screen A 13.4,
screen B 3.73 (`evidence/e5_scarp_structure.json`). It is registered as the natural next build
(needs a mass-restoration rule that is justified, not invented), and it is **not** cleared.

## 8. Why the ceiling is where it is, and the only way to raise it

The metric reduces to `1/DTI = α + α(F/T) + β(K/T)`. At the live anchor the champion's emission sits
within ~10 % of the point where the marginal dot's credit equals the bar `α·DTI` — the generator
stopped where theory says it should. Every remaining lever is therefore *knowledge of where the
hidden faults are*, not emission geometry, and no local proxy can rank candidate fields (this
session reproduced that failure a third time, §2). The arithmetic in `knowledge/02` still holds and
is the highest-value action available to the operator: three slots — anchor, λ = 0.5 rescale,
null-add 2,000 dots — identify `T`, `K` and `F` exactly from the returned leaderboard numbers, cost
no standing (both probes are constructed below the anchor, and only the best submission counts), and
convert every decision in this repository from a projection into a measurement. **No slot was spent
this session**; the operator's standing is unchanged.

## 11. Limitations (carried forward)

1. No ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED score exists for any artifact in this repository. Every live number is
   OWNER-REPORT.
2. Screens A and B are n = 6, threshold-sensitive, and share one inversion; they are family-level
   instruments, not rankers.
3. The XCRD field's independence rests on the SGMC positivity proxy; if SGMC off-catalogue traces
   are not fault-like, the field's own objective is wrong (a leakage-style risk that only the
   identification experiment can price).
4. The ring-peel projection assumes the 1 px and 2 px rings are representative of the 3 px ring.
5. `h52_scarp3m_100m.tif` is a validation channel here, never a feature; the scarp bands are
   *counts*, and reading them as masks (band > 0) inflates the evidence set 12× — a bug this session
   found and fixed in `scripts/e2_xcres.py`.
