New this session (2026-10-11): H62-1 … H62-5, ranked by expected DTI improvement vs implementation cost

The top candidate H62-1 was preregistered (registry/preregistration-h62.json, written before any result) and validated on the spatially-blocked holdout — verdict and numbers are on the front page and in evidence/h62_validation.json.   5. H62-5 (rank 5) — true-10 m χ/ksn concordance (the tectonic-geomorphology lane at its required resolution; USGS 3DEP 10 m, public domain — blocked from this sandbox, pins + Actions receipt exist).
