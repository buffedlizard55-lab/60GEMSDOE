Why the highest GEMSDOE score is 0.2778

It is a pruning result, not a model result. h33-h33-2-b2 is the owner-reported 0.2708 raster with 2,545 dots deleted and none added; every removed dot was 1.4–2.0 px from the mapped catalogue. Source: the family's own evidence/session6_mechanism_and_witness.json (equals_base_pruned_at_2px: true).

The metric reduces exactly to 1/DTI = α + α·(F/T) + β·(|G|/T), so adding one unit of mass raises DTI iff its realised kernel credit exceeds α·DTI — 0.052 at DTI = 0.26, i.e. the dot must land within ~284 m of a hidden truth pixel. Every deleted dot failed that test.

Beating the leader is therefore a field problem: holding mass and the hidden |G| fixed, the retained credit T has to rise about +45 %. This repository does not claim to have done that, and nothing here is written as a score.

What unblocks the lane

The lane asks for a 3DEP 10 m DEM. This sandbox cannot reach the official USGS bucket: curl https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com returns HTTP 000 (measured, see evidence/connectivity.json). The only absolute-elevation grid obtainable here is the GeoDAWN 100 m DEM, and at 100 m the base-level step the method looks for is not resolved — which is the most likely cause of the STOP verdict.

scripts/fetch_3dep_10m.sh is written, probes the host, and refuses to claim success it did not get. Run it where egress is normal; the rest of the lane (src/gems60/*) then runs unchanged.

Every number on this site is labelled HOLDOUT-DTI (evaluator version, number of withheld positives, 95 % CI), ORGANIZER-PUBLISHED, OWNER-REPORTED or MEASURED. A projection is never written as a score. Nothing here has been submitted to the competition by this project.
